# Inputs and rights

## Own material

The checkout fixture, original source, contract cases, UI and generated graphics are
our work. The fixture deliberately demonstrates a discount/shipping defect. It does
not represent a production customer incident.

The paid sample was generated from our source and specification. Its code is labelled
provider-generated. Evidence records the actual request, raw answer, commitment and
settlement. It contains no private customer data or protected evaluation questions.
